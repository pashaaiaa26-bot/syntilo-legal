2. Information we collect

Information you give us:

  • Your name, phone number and WhatsApp profile name
  • Delivery address, city and any delivery instructions
  • Order details: products, quantities, prices and order history
  • Messages, voice notes, images or files you send us, and your questions or feedback

Information collected automatically:

  • Message metadata provided by WhatsApp, such as message time and delivery/read status

What we do not collect: we never ask for your Easypaisa or JazzCash PIN, password, OTP or card details. If anyone asks for these in our name, please do not share them and report it to us.

3. How we use your information

  • To answer your questions and provide customer support
  • To take, confirm, process, deliver and track your orders
  • To verify payments and handle cash-on-delivery, returns and refunds
  • To send order updates, delivery notifications and service messages you requested
  • To send offers and promotional messages only where you have agreed to receive them; you can opt out at any time
  • To improve our products, service quality and customer experience
  • To prevent fraud and misuse, and to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations

4. WhatsApp and automated replies

We communicate with customers through the WhatsApp Business Platform, provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. Messages exchanged with us on WhatsApp are also subject to WhatsApp's Privacy Policy.

Some replies on our WhatsApp number may be generated by an automated assistant to help you faster, including outside business hours. The assistant uses the information in your message and our product and policy information to prepare a reply. You can ask to speak with a member of our team at any time, and important matters such as complaints, refunds or order problems are handled by our staff.

5. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed with:

  • Delivery and courier partners - your name, phone number and address, to deliver your order
  • Payment providers (Easypaisa, JazzCash) - to receive and verify your payment
  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram) - to send and receive messages and operate our pages
  • Technology service providers - such as hosting, software and AI service providers that help us run our systems and automated replies, under obligations to protect your information
  • Authorities - where required by law, or to protect our rights, customers or the public

6. How long we keep your information

  • Order and payment records: for as long as required for accounting, tax and legal purposes
  • Chat messages and support history: for up to 12 months after your last interaction with us, unless needed longer to resolve a complaint or dispute
  • Marketing preferences: until you opt out or ask us to delete them

When information is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

7. How we protect your information

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including access controls, secure connections and limiting access to staff and service providers who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and to respond promptly to any issue.
